# Strategy Update — 2026-04-08

## Context

First full analysis of live trading data from `data/trades.jsonl` (487 unique trades, 2026-04-07).
Net result: **-$22.76**. Strategy was underwater primarily due to a broken exit mechanism.

---

## Problems Identified

### 1. Lock-gain firing on near-zero profit (root cause of -$14.87)

The fee formula in `exits.ts` is correct per Polymarket docs:

```
fee = C × feeRate × p × (1 - p)
```

where C = shares, feeRate from `/fee-rate` endpoint, p = price.

However, the `/fee-rate` endpoint was returning 0 (or a shape where `base_fee` was missing),
causing `feeRateBps` to fall back to 0. With zero fees, **any** `profitPerShare > 0` cleared
the fee check — including positions where bestBid had barely ticked up by 0.001. 349 lock_gain
sells fired on noise.

The fee formula itself was not changed — it was always correct. The fallback to 0 is the hazard.

**Fix:** Added two percentage-based floors that gate lock_gain independent of fee calculations.
Hard numbers (e.g. 2¢/share, $0.50 notional) don't scale — 2¢ on a 0.10 token is a 20% move
requirement but only 2% on a 0.90 token. Percentages scale correctly across the price range:

- `MIN_LOCK_GAIN_PRICE_PCT` = 0.05 — price must improve by ≥5% of `avgPrice`
  (0.50 entry → needs 0.525 bid; 0.10 entry → needs 0.105 bid)
- `MIN_LOCK_GAIN_RETURN_PCT` = 0.03 — gross profit must be ≥3% of position cost (`avgPrice × size`)

### 2. No minimum per-share or notional floor on lock_gain

Even with a correct fee formula, a 5-share position at 0.52 moving to 0.53 earns $0.05 gross.
The sell order costs more in spread slippage than the gain. Lock_gain was firing on noise.

**Fix:** Added two new config guards (both must pass):
- `MIN_LOCK_GAIN_PER_SHARE` = 0.02 (2¢/share minimum price improvement)
- `MIN_LOCK_GAIN_NOTIONAL` = 0.50 ($0.50 minimum gross profit)

### 3. Tiny positions bleeding fees

59.8% of trades had |PnL| < $0.06. Positions of 4–5 shares at prices 0.20–0.40
(notional $0.80–$2.00) can't absorb bid-ask spread + fees and still be profitable.
The minimum notional floor on lock_gain partially addresses this. Full fix requires
a minimum notional filter at entry (not yet implemented — see Open Items).

### 4. edgeAtEntry coverage at 13.6%

Only 66 of 487 trades had `edgeAtEntry` populated. The edge↔profit correlation was
r = -0.235 (slightly negative), but this is not meaningful at low coverage.
Cannot evaluate Kelly sizing effectiveness without full edge tracking.

## What We Expect

- lock_gain exits drop from ~349 to a small fraction — only fire when price has genuinely
  improved by ≥2¢/share AND total gain ≥ $0.50.
- Most previously-lock_gain positions will now hold until `take_profit` (≥2¢, from `MIN_EDGE`),
  `loss_cut` (≤-2¢), or `underwater_persistence` (5 consecutive no-edge checks).
- Net PnL should improve as fewer marginal sells are dispatched.
